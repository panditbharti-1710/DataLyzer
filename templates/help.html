Help & Support

Find answers and learn how to use DataLyzer

📂 Uploading Data

Upload CSV files only. Make sure your file is not empty and contains proper column headers.

📊 Creating Visualizations

Select chart type, choose columns, and click Generate Visualization to view your data graphically.

🧹 Data Cleaning

DataLyzer automatically handles missing values, removes duplicates, and corrects data types.

🔐 Privacy & Security

Sensitive columns like names and emails are detected and masked automatically when privacy is enabled.

🤖 NLP Insights

Enable NLP in Settings to generate human-readable summaries of your analysis.

Data Overview

🎥Data Overview shows a quick summary of your dataset, including columns, missing values, duplicates, data types, and a preview of the data before analysis.
